Case Study: Ecommerce Website Development

WebMentr helped a growing retail business launch a professional ecommerce website that allowed them to sell products online and reach customers beyond their local market.

Project Background

The client previously sold products only through a physical store and social media. They needed a dedicated ecommerce website where customers could browse products, view prices and place orders.

Challenges

  • No existing ecommerce website
  • Limited product visibility
  • Manual order management
  • Lack of online branding

Solution

WebMentr developed a responsive ecommerce website with product catalog, category navigation and a simple checkout system. The website was optimized for mobile users and designed to load quickly across all devices.

Results

  • Increased product visibility
  • Online orders from new customers
  • Improved brand credibility
  • Better customer experience
